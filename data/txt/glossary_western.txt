# HealthFit Western Medicine Glossary
# Format: #ID | Term | English | Definition | Related Role

#001 | BMI | Body Mass Index | Body mass index, weight (kg) ÷ height² (m), used to assess whether body weight is normal. <18.5 underweight, 18.5-24 normal, >24 overweight, >28 obese | Dr. Mei

#002 | BMR | Basal Metabolic Rate | Basal metabolic rate, the minimum calories required to maintain vital signs in a completely resting state (kcal/day) | Dr. Mei

#003 | TDEE | Total Daily Energy Expenditure | Total daily energy expenditure, BMR × activity factor, the basis for creating a diet plan | Dr. Mei

#004 | 1RM | One-Rep Max | One-repetition maximum, the maximum load that can be completed once for a given movement, used to calculate training intensity | Coach Alex

#005 | RPE | Rate of Perceived Exertion | Subjective perceived exertion, scored 1-10, a subjective indicator for measuring training intensity | Coach Alex

#006 | DOMS | Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness | Delayed onset muscle soreness, muscle soreness that appears 24-72 hours after training; a normal physiological response | Coach Alex

#007 | Concentric Contraction | Concentric Contraction | A contraction method in which the muscle shortens to produce force, such as the pressing-up phase of the bench press | Coach Alex

#008 | Eccentric Contraction | Eccentric Contraction | Muscle lengthens while producing force, such as the lowering phase of the bench press; causes greater muscle damage | Coach Alex

#009 | Supercompensation | Supercompensation | The phenomenon in which the body recovers after training to a level above the original level; the physiological basis of muscle growth | Coach Alex

#010 | Protein Synthesis Window | Anabolic Window | The period within 30-60 minutes after exercise when protein synthesis efficiency is relatively high | Dr. Mei

#011 | GI | Glycemic Index | Glycemic index, an indicator of how quickly food raises blood glucose; low-GI foods are more beneficial for fat loss | Dr. Mei

#012 | HIIT | High-Intensity Interval Training | High-intensity interval training, alternating short periods of high-intensity exercise and rest; efficient for fat burning | Coach Alex

#013 | LISS | Low-Intensity Steady State | Low-intensity steady-state aerobic exercise, such as jogging and brisk walking, suitable for beginners and recovery days | Coach Alex

#014 | Hypertrophy | Hypertrophy | Enlargement of muscle fibers, usually stimulated through moderate-weight training for 8-12 repetitions | Coach Alex

#015 | Progressive Overload | Progressive Overload | Gradually increasing training load (weight/reps/sets) to continuously stimulate muscle growth | Coach Alex

#016 | Compound Movement | Compound Movement | A movement involving multiple joints and muscle groups, such as squat, deadlift, and bench press | Coach Alex

#017 | Isolation Movement | Isolation Movement | A movement that mainly targets a single muscle group, such as biceps curl | Coach Alex

#018 | Caloric Surplus | Caloric Surplus | Calorie intake > calorie expenditure, the state required during the muscle-gain phase | Dr. Mei

#019 | Caloric Deficit | Caloric Deficit | Calorie intake < calorie expenditure, the state required during the fat-loss phase | Dr. Mei

#020 | Macronutrients | Macronutrients | The three major nutrients: protein, carbohydrates, and fat | Dr. Mei

#021 | Micronutrients | Micronutrients | Vitamins and minerals; required in small amounts but crucial to health | Dr. Mei

#022 | Heart Rate Reserve | Heart Rate Reserve | Maximum heart rate - resting heart rate, used to calculate target training heart rate | Coach Alex

#023 | VO2max | Maximal Oxygen Uptake | Maximal oxygen uptake, the gold-standard indicator for measuring cardiopulmonary function | Analyst Ray

#024 | Core Stability | Core Stability | The ability of abdominal, back, and pelvic-floor muscle groups to coordinate and maintain spinal stability | Coach Alex

#025 | Functional Training | Functional Training | Training that simulates daily-life movement patterns and improves practical movement ability | Coach Alex

#026 | Metabolic Adaptation | Metabolic Adaptation | The phenomenon of basal metabolism decreasing after long-term calorie restriction, commonly called a "plateau" | Dr. Mei

#027 | Refeed Day | Refeed Day | A day during fat loss when carbohydrate intake is occasionally increased to relieve metabolic adaptation | Dr. Mei

#028 | Body Fat Percentage | Body Fat Percentage | The percentage of body weight made up by body fat mass; reflects body composition better than BMI | Analyst Ray
